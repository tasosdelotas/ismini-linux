❓ Help & Shortcuts

ismini is a personal AI agent that runs 100% locally. Everything below works offline — your chats, memory, and models never leave your machine.

🌐 Visit the ismini website — features, screenshots, and downloads.

🌐 Recommended browser: Microsoft Edge (best Text-to-Speech voices), then Google Chrome. ismini opens one of them automatically — Edge first.

Chat

EnterSend the message.
Shift + EnterAdd a new line in the message box.
EscapeClose an open window (Models, Help, popovers).
⏸ PauseStop ismini mid-answer and redirect it. Appears while it is working.

Header buttons

Model listPick a downloaded model to load. The ✅ marks the active model.
k ctxContext length — how much of the conversation the model can see at once (history + your message + its reply). Click the chip to change it (16k → 256k). The default is 16k. A bigger context = more memory needed. ismini keeps your conversation inside the limit automatically.
sudo: ON/OFFAllow ismini to run shell commands with sudo. Keep OFF unless a task needs it.
🔊 TTSToggle Text-to-Speech — ismini reads its replies aloud.
🎤 LiveContinuous hands-free voice conversation mode.
🤖 ModelsOpen the Model Manager to download AI models.
Switch modelChange models on the fly, even mid-conversation — just pick a new one from the model list at the top.
Auto-detectismini finds models you already have in LM Studio and adds them to the list automatically — no setup needed.
❓ HelpThis window.
⚙️ SettingsTTS voice, speech-recognition language, and HuggingFace token.
ThemesPapyrus, Stars, or Marble look.
SessionsSwitch between your current conversation and any archived one (unlimited). Click ✕ to delete a session, or "Clear all" to wipe them all.
New chatArchive the current thread and start a fresh conversation.
UpdateCheck for a newer ismini release.
🪟 Window sizeResize the ismini window however you like — ismini remembers it. It re-opens at your size (centered on screen) next time you launch. Your size is saved to your install, so it's private to you.

Message-box buttons

📄Pick a file on your PC and insert its path into the chat.
📁Pick a folder and insert its path.
🖼️Attach an image (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF) for ismini to see. Requires a Vision model.
🎤 MicVoice dictation — speak, and your words appear in the box.
SendSend the message (same as Enter).

What ismini can do

FilesRead, write, edit, and delete files — limited to your home folder and workspace. Sensitive paths are blocked.
ShellRun shell commands. Destructive or dangerous commands ask you to confirm first.
WebSearch the web and read pages for up-to-date answers.
MemorySay “remember this” and ismini saves the fact to recall it in future sessions.

Uninstall

./uninstall.shSafely stops the server and removes the app, desktop icons, and temporary files.

⚠️ This also deletes your downloaded models and chat history. Back them up first if you need them!
